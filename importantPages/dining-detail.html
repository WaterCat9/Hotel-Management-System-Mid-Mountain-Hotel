Signature Restaurants

Four extraordinary dining destinations — each a universe of flavor, each a testament to culinary artistry

Underwater Fine Dining

Ossiano

Descend into an ethereal underwater realm where floor-to-ceiling aquariums frame an intimate dining experience. Awarded three Michelin stars, Ossiano pairs avant-garde gastronomy with the mesmerizing beauty of the deep. Every dish is a visual and sensory masterpiece, crafted with the world's rarest seafood and seasonal treasures.

Garden to Table

The Forest

Nestled among ancient trees and blooming gardens, The Forest celebrates the harmony of nature and gastronomy. Ingredients are harvested daily from our organic gardens and sourced from trusted local purveyors. Seasonal menus reflect the rhythm of the land — each plate a tribute to nature's abundance, prepared with reverence and simplicity.

Artisanal Pizzeria

Pizza Church

Housed in a beautifully restored chapel, Pizza Church elevates Italy's most beloved export to celestial heights. Hand-stretched dough undergoes a 72-hour fermentation before meeting heritage San Marzano tomatoes and hand-pulled mozzarella di bufala. Each pizza is a prayer to perfection — kissed by flame, blessed by tradition.

Global Fusion

TIKKI

A vibrant celebration of global flavors, TIKKI brings together culinary traditions from every corner of the world. From Mexican street food to Southeast Asian treasures, from American smokehouse to avant-garde creations — each dish transcends borders. Bold, innovative, and unforgettable, TIKKI redefines what fusion cuisine can be.
