Five-Star Amenities

A curated collection of leisure and wellness experiences — each designed to enrich your stay and elevate your spirit

Aquatic Serenity

Swimming Pool

Our stunning infinity-edge pool appears to merge with the mountain horizon, offering a swim experience unlike any other. The heated Olympic-length pool is surrounded by private cabanas, each equipped with plush loungers and dedicated beverage service. Underwater speakers fill the water with ambient melodies, while the adjacent jacuzzi and cold-plunge pool complete a world-class hydrotherapy circuit.

Holistic Wellness

SPA

Step into a sanctuary of tranquility where ancient healing traditions meet modern wellness science. Our award-winning SPA spans over 2,000 square meters and features twelve treatment suites, each with private steam and shower facilities. Indulge in signature therapies — from Himalayan salt stone massages to gold-infused facials — all delivered by master therapists using organic, sustainably sourced products.

Private Entertainment

KTV

Unleash your inner star in our state-of-the-art KTV suites. Each suite is a private entertainment sanctuary — featuring professional-grade acoustics, an extensive multilingual song library updated weekly, and immersive LED wall displays. Premium audio equipment from Bang & Olufsen ensures every note rings true. A dedicated host, gourmet bar menu, and customizable lighting themes complete the experience.

Peak Performance

GYM

Our 24-hour fitness center rivals the world's finest private clubs. Equipped with Technogym's latest Artis line — from adaptive treadmills to biometric resistance machines — every workout is intelligent and personalized. Floor-to-ceiling windows overlook the mountain panorama, while personal trainers certified by international academies stand ready to design bespoke programs. Yoga and Pilates studios offer daily classes in sun-drenched spaces.

Sophisticated Leisure

Cards Room

A refined space for those who appreciate the art of the game. Our Cards Room evokes the ambiance of an exclusive private club — rich wood paneling, leather club chairs, and soft amber lighting create an atmosphere of timeless sophistication. Professional dealers host bridge, poker, and mahjong sessions upon request. A curated selection of rare whiskies, premium cigars, and artisanal chocolates is available to complement your evening.

World-Class Facilities

From the serenity of our infinity pool to the vibrant energy of KTV, from therapeutic spa journeys to high-performance workouts — every facility at Mid-Mountain Hotel is designed to enrich, restore, and delight. Your leisure is our passion, and every moment here is crafted to be unforgettable.
