🧹 Hotel Cleaning

Cleanliness is the heart of hospitality. Our cleaning management module assigns tasks to dedicated staff, tracks progress in real time, and ensures every corner of every room meets our exacting standards. From routine tidying to deep disinfection, our streamlined workflow guarantees a spotless, welcoming environment for every guest, every single day.

Minimize downtime with our rapid repair module. Submit requests directly through the system and our maintenance team receives them instantly. Track the repair status from report to resolution, ensuring every facility – from air conditioning to lighting – is operating at peak performance. Transparent timelines and quality guarantees keep your hotel running smoothly.

Never let essential supplies run low. Our intelligent inventory system sends automatic restock alerts when items fall below threshold levels. Manage toiletries, linens, and amenities with just a few clicks, and keep detailed records for future planning. Standardized material management reduces waste and ensures every room is fully equipped before guests arrive.

✦ Auto low-stock alerts · Customizable supply lists

😊 Living Feedback

Your guests' voices shape our service. The feedback module collects reviews and suggestions effortlessly, allowing you to respond quickly and improve continuously. Data analytics highlight trends and areas for enhancement, while automated follow‑ups show guests that their opinions truly matter.
